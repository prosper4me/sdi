Anchor Account Deletion

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

This page explains how users of Anchor, the AI-powered relationship coaching application operated by Systems Direct Industries, LLC, can request deletion of their Anchor account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to dhprovendo@gmail.com using the email address associated with your Anchor account. Put Anchor Account Deletion in the subject line.

What to include:
  • The email address associated with your Anchor account.
  • A statement that you want your Anchor account and associated personal data deleted.

What Will Be Deleted

Upon processing a valid deletion request, we will delete the Anchor account and associated account information that we control, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and record-retention requirements.

Account information may include the account email address and authentication information associated with the Anchor account. Information held by third-party service providers may be subject to their applicable deletion and retention policies.

Conversation Data

Conversation information stored locally on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Anchor. Data that has been transmitted to third-party services may be retained or deleted according to the applicable provider's policies and our applicable requirements.

Processing Requests

We may need to verify that the request is associated with the account owner before processing a deletion request. We will review and respond to valid requests as required by applicable law.
