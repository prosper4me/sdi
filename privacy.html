Anchor Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

Systems Direct Industries, LLC ("Systems Direct," "we," "us," or "our") operates Anchor, an AI-powered relationship coaching application for women. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Anchor.

1. Information We May Process

Information You Provide

When you use Anchor's coaching features, you may provide information in messages, questions, situations, or other content that you choose to enter into the app. Please do not provide highly sensitive information that is not necessary to receive coaching.

Conversation Information

Anchor may store conversation history locally on your device so that you can return to conversations and continue where you left off. This information is associated with the app data on your device.

Subscription and Purchase Information

Anchor uses RevenueCat and the applicable app store to manage subscriptions and determine whether a user has access to premium features. Payment information such as full credit-card numbers is handled by the applicable app store and is not directly collected by Anchor.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through Anchor may be used to:

  • Provide relationship coaching and conversational guidance;
  • Generate responses to questions and messages submitted through AI features;
  • Maintain conversation continuity on your device;
  • Provide and manage premium subscription access;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.

3. AI Processing

When you use Anchor's AI coaching features, the content you submit may be transmitted to an AI service provider so that an AI-generated response can be produced. You should avoid entering information that you would not want processed by a third-party technology provider.

Anchor does not use your coaching conversations for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Anchor uses third-party services necessary to provide app functionality, including AI processing and subscription management. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • AI service provider: used to generate AI coaching responses.
  • RevenueCat: used to manage subscription status and premium entitlements.
  • Google Play: used for Android app distribution and subscription purchases.

5. Information We Do Not Sell

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through Anchor. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Conversation information stored locally by Anchor remains on your device until it is removed by the app, the app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their applicable policies and contractual requirements.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information associated with Anchor, contact us using the email address below. We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Anchor is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Anchor, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page. Continued use of Anchor after an updated policy becomes effective means that the updated policy applies to your use of the app.
